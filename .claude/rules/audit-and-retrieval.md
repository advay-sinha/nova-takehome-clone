# Audit and retrieval invariants

Retrieve all applicable governing records, authority provisions and financial records before semantic interpretation. Semantic top-k is supplementary. Verify source receipt time, effective time, entity, contract, shipment, lane, charge and feedback scope separately.

Do not prefilter away conflicting signed/authorized sources. Similarity does not establish precedence, authorize a waiver, identify a duplicate by itself, or grant permission to execute an action. Expand retrieved fragments to complete parent clauses and tables where needed.

Keep exact doc/page/quote provenance. Use about 180-220 tokenizer tokens for MiniLM search chunks and prevent silent encoder truncation. Preserve original text separately from normalized values.

Use typed, cited RAG proposals with bounded calls and cache keys covering all evidence and policy versions. Any new relevant amendment/credit/payment/feedback invalidates affected interpretation caches. Python computes final financial results and policy outcomes.

Hold runtime data in `--state-dir`; ship pretrained encoder/tokenizer files with tested versions/checksums/license. Do not rely on a home-directory cache, evaluator internet access, GPU, embedding service or unresolved LFS pointer.

No custom training by default. If considered, use the human-execution rule and keep the existing deterministic audit path.

Check observed document arithmetic without forcing contract compliance. A targeted model recheck is bounded and source-validated, never a vote or an authority signal. Keep internal typed calculation/action traces under state and verify their consistency without extending the official report schema.

Follow `references/DIFFERENTIATORS.md`: six metamorphic families, family-held-out ablations, optional cross-encoder off until measured gates pass, and no learned confidence/custom training in the current scope.
